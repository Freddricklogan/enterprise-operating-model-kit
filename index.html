            Ninety-day playbook

            How the three instruments are used together, in order.

            Days 1–30 · Diagnose

            1. List the capabilities the strategy depends on; score importance, maturity and target with the people who run them.
            2. List the decisions that matter; record who decides, recommends, gives input and is informed — from practice, not the org chart.
            3. Collect the objectives every unit already has.

            Days 31–60 · Decide

            1. Take the top five capability gaps to the leadership team and fund at most three.
            2. Fix every RACI finding: one decider per decision, a named recommender.
            3. Rebuild the cascade so every objective contributes upward and carries a key result; retire the rest.

            Days 61–90 · Operate

            1. Re-score maturity monthly; the heat-map becomes a trend.
            2. Publish the RACI; refer to it in every escalation.
            3. Review key results monthly, cascade alignment quarterly.
